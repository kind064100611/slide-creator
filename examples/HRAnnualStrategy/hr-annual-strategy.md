- **10%**

## Evidence-based assessment model

Past performance remains part of the assessment, but the model places greater weight on three forward-looking dimensions.

- **01** Learning agility
- **02** Adaptability to change
- **03** Leadership potential

## Targeted retention strategy

### Executive mentorship
VP-level leaders hold regular career and development conversations with selected employees.

### Cross-functional rotations
Rotations broaden operating experience and strengthen business capability.

### Accelerated promotion path
Exceptional employees can advance based on readiness rather than tenure.

## High productivity standard
- **1.913**
- million RMB revenue per employee
- The target exceeds the national benchmark for large industrial enterprises.

## High-potential talent club

Cross-industry exchanges and advanced training strengthen belonging, recognition, and development.

## Voluntary attrition target
- **≤1%**, well below the industry average
- Protect critical knowledge and maintain a reliable leadership pipeline.
