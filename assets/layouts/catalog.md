# Layout catalog

This catalog contains only the fixed page structures required by the current example suite. A layout is one complete slide skeleton, not a component library. Match by authored structure and relationships, never by business topic, theme color, or loose keyword similarity.

## Matching procedure

1. Resolve the Markdown slide-boundary level before lookup. Route exactly one catalog layout for each boundary-level source owner; lower-level headings remain regions inside that owner.
2. Describe the owner's direct regions, nested parent paths, peer counts, metric counts, table dimensions, and connector edges. Preserve every authored region and its order.
3. Eliminate every row whose **Reject when** condition applies. The lookup recommendation is only a retrieval hint.
4. Choose one final fixed skeleton. Do not combine layouts, introduce new region types, or turn relationship nodes into generic cards.
5. Variability is allowed only inside a declared repeat region. Emit direct child nodes that satisfy that region's `data-layout-item-*` contract; do not nest another template, compose layouts, add slots, or invent region types.
6. After all routes are final, read each unique selected layout file once and retain only its compact structure summary. Do not read candidate HTML files during normal generation.
7. If no retained skeleton matches, split only at a source-owned boundary. Do not invent a cover, contents page, divider, closing page, focus block, or summary band.

## Hard routing rules

These rules are mutually exclusive gates, not suggestions. Record why the selected row passes and why every superficially similar row is rejected before reading the final template file.

- `dashboard` requires all four roles: page-level assessment, a repeatable peer headline-measure group, one same-unit comparison, and two named detail/action groups. Numbers plus prose are insufficient.
- An authored timestamped incident sequence with metrics and a bottom causal conclusion routes to `timeline-metric-chain-conclusion`.
- A lead with one title and repeatable peer narrative paragraphs plus two semantic tiers of same-kind metric cards routes to `metric-grid`; only paragraph and metric-card nodes are repeated. A tier boundary may be explicit in the source or evident from an ordered schema change such as concrete product observations followed by forecasts, aggregate ratios, or business implications. Preserve that boundary instead of flattening all peers into one card group.
- A quantified-loss owner with one lead loss, a grouped lead-fact strip, four homogeneous loss cards, one compound multi-metric damage group, one separate recovery-cost group, and a page-level warning routes to `metric-grid-support-warning`. This is not `metric-grid`: the compound support group and warning are different authored schemas, not a second tier of homogeneous metric cards.
- A compact lead followed by a semantic table routes to `flex-table`; the lead and table stay on one page.
- Peer columns ordered as title, value/caption, two-or-more evidence statements, and a distinct conclusion/direction sentence route to `list-metric-note-peer-columns`; column and evidence-item counts come from the source. This remains the match when a page-level lead precedes the peers. A lead mark must remain an authored icon/label rather than a promoted peer metric.
- A full-width summary containing one title, one narrative paragraph, and one metric cluster followed by a repeatable evidence-card group routes to `summary-band-evidence-cards`. Each evidence card owns a title, one grouped label/value/caption highlight, and one separate explanatory paragraph; the highlight facts are not repeated in the paragraph.
- A side root with an explicit fan-out into repeated branches plus an independent right note stack routes to `branch-hierarchy-with-notes`.
- Heading hierarchy is relationship evidence: one governing H2/root followed by repeated H3 result branches and then multiple independent H2 note groups is a side-root hierarchy with notes even when the Markdown contains no arrow glyph. It must route to `branch-hierarchy-with-notes`, not to `ordered-control-grid-goal-band`.
- A timestamped timeline whose authored bottom band contains a left conclusion label, a causal chain, and right explanatory copy routes to `timeline-metric-chain-conclusion`; emit each chain node as a direct child matching the declared item contract and retain the template-owned shaft and arrowhead between adjacent nodes.
- A quantified-loss page with a top metric strip, four same-kind loss cards, one compound supporting brand card, one single recovery-cost support card, and one warning band routes to `metric-grid-support-warning`.
- A root-cause banner followed by a labeled process row and a labeled review-cause row routes to `lead-analysis-process-review-grid`.
- A four-card ordered control grid with card-owned goals plus one full-width overall target band routes to `ordered-control-grid-goal-band`.
- A four-peer page ending in an ordinary summary/takeaway routes to `card-row-summary`. `ordered-control-grid-goal-band` is valid only when every peer owns an explicit local goal/direction and the final band is explicitly an overall goal/target; a generic `Summary` is not an overall goal.
- A four-card emergency response flow with one featured SLA stack and one MTTR/target band routes to `card-flow-target-band`.
- A year-marked talent strategy page with one metric intro card, repeated assessment factors, repeated strategy cards, and repeated sidebar notes routes to `metric-assessment-strategy-side-stack`.
- Three parallel incentive columns with two evidence subsections, one highlighted value, and one outcome quote each route to `metric-highlight-quote-columns`.
- A three-column ROI summary band followed by three homogeneous ROI evidence cards routes to `summary-metric-peer-cards`.
- A metric-leading historical summary band followed by repeatable history cards with metric panels routes to `metric-summary-history-cards`; a paired comparison remains two labeled values separated inside its panel.

## Retained fixed structures

| Layout | Match signals from the source | Reject when | Presentation role |
|---|---|---|---|
| [dashboard](dashboard.html) | One assessment/lead, a repeatable headline-measure group, one compact same-unit comparison occupying no more than one quarter of slide width, and two named detail/action groups; numbered source detail items stay ordered and the terminal action note stays in the footer | Any required role is absent; values use incompatible units; the comparison becomes a half-page chart; footer content is duplicated into the body; a relationship, table, or sequence dominates | Account or performance dashboard |
| [evidence-impact](evidence-impact.html) | A repeatable group of comparable signal cards, one ordered evidence timeline, and one independent repeatable impact group with no inferred cross-column pairing connectors | Signals are not comparable; evidence is not ordered; the impact group is absent or merged into a signal, or arrows would imply unsupported one-to-one timeline-to-impact relationships | Evidence-to-impact analysis |
| [action-plan](action-plan.html) | A repeatable group of ordered action horizons with actions and ownership, plus one source-owned scorecard/action note containing repeatable measures | Workstreams are descriptive peers, ownership is absent, or more region types would have to be invented | Accountable recovery plan |
| [metric-grid](metric-grid.html) | One required lead band with one title and repeatable peer paragraphs, followed by two semantic tiers of same-kind metric groups; every card has title, value, and note. Use when the ordered peers change from concrete observations/products to forecasts, aggregate ratios, or implications even if the source does not spell out visual tier names | All peers belong to one homogeneous semantic tier; the ordered schema never changes; cards have different internal schemas; a compound multi-metric support group or page-level warning is authored; or a table, flow, or hierarchy dominates | Dense two-tier metric overview |
| [card-row-summary](card-row-summary.html) | A repeatable group of independent peer cards followed by one compact full-width source-owned ordinary summary/takeaway | Cards are sequential or connected, the summary is absent, card schemas differ, each card owns a separate goal, or the final band is explicitly an overall goal/target | Peer scan with takeaway |
| [flex-table](flex-table.html) | One compact source-owned lead/note followed by a 2-5 column semantic table with 2-7 rows | The table is not primary, the lead is absent, or sibling regions need another topology | Lead plus semantic table |
| [list-metric-note-peer-columns](list-metric-note-peer-columns.html) | An optional compact source-owned lead followed by a repeatable peer-column group; each column owns title, value/caption, two-or-more evidence statements, and a distinct compact conclusion/direction note | Role order differs, the peers contain only one compact copy block, a page summary is authored, any note belongs outside its peer, or a lead metric would be promoted from a peer column | Evidence/value/conclusion peer columns |
| [summary-band-evidence-cards](summary-band-evidence-cards.html) | One summary band with one title, one narrative paragraph, and one metric cluster, followed by repeatable same-schema cards; each card preserves a standalone centered ordinal, title, grouped label/value/caption highlight, and immediately following explanatory copy without a dead middle band | The ordinal is merged into the title; the summary is a peer card; a card-owned value is promoted to the summary; highlight facts are duplicated into the body; cards are generic bullet panels; or the page lacks the summary-band-plus-analysis structure | Forecast or decision-window summary over analysis cards |
| [branch-hierarchy-with-notes](branch-hierarchy-with-notes.html) | One side root, template-owned smooth fan-out connectors with compact attached arrowheads, a repeatable middle branch stack, and an independent repeatable right note stack. A named root/target plus four result branches and separate page-level notes maps here even when the Markdown expresses the relation through headings rather than arrow glyphs | The source instead authors four independent controls each with its own goal plus a full-width overall goal band; connectors are absent; a side-note metric would replace the root; the root is above the branches; or notes are branch children | Side-root hierarchy with independent notes |
| [timeline-metric-chain-conclusion](timeline-metric-chain-conclusion.html) | One timestamped event sequence; each event owns metric chips and explanation; one bottom conclusion band owns a label, canonical repeated chain nodes, template-owned shafts and compact attached arrowheads, and explanatory copy | The chain is flattened, events are not timestamped, connector objects are omitted, or arrow glyphs replace the template-owned geometry | Timeline with metric cards and causal conclusion |
| [metric-grid-support-warning](metric-grid-support-warning.html) | Top quantified loss area with exactly one divider between one lead metric and one grouped fact set, four same-kind loss cards, one compound multi-metric supporting group, one single recovery-cost support group, and one page-level warning band; repeated nodes obey the declared item contracts and ordinary labels remain non-heading text | Extra dividers appear between facts, the lead label or warning is promoted to a title, the support row is absent, peer cards are not homogeneous, or the warning is card-owned instead of page-owned | Quantified loss overview |
| [lead-analysis-process-review-grid](lead-analysis-process-review-grid.html) | A root-cause banner with peer facts, followed by a labeled process-card row and a labeled review-card row | Either labeled tier is absent, process cards are not ordered peers, or review causes are merged into the root banner | Root cause plus process/review tiers |
| [metric-strip-chain-notes](metric-strip-chain-notes.html) | Four headline status metrics with ordinary small labels and prominent values over a two-column body with one vertical mechanism chain and three numbered evidence notes; repeated nodes obey their declared item contracts and retain one template-owned shaft plus attached arrowhead between adjacent mechanism boxes | Status strip, mechanism chain, or note stack is absent; connectors are omitted or rendered as arrow glyphs; an ordinary metric label is promoted to a heading; notes are peer columns rather than stacked evidence | Metric strip with mechanism chain and notes |
| [ordered-control-grid-goal-band](ordered-control-grid-goal-band.html) | Four ordered peer control cards; every card owns a checklist plus an explicit local goal/direction; one bottom band is explicitly authored as the overall goal/target | The source has one governing root followed by four H3 branches and independent H2 notes; the final band is merely a summary/takeaway; local goals are absent; goals are page-level peers; cards are connected/sequential; or the bottom overall-goal band is absent | Ordered controls with overall goal |
| [card-flow-target-band](card-flow-target-band.html) | A source-authored horizontal response flow of peer stage cards with template-owned explicit arrows between adjacent stages; one featured card may own a three-item SLA stack, followed by one bottom MTTR/target band | More than one card has nested steps, direction is not authored, or the target band is absent | Emergency response flow with target band |
| [metric-assessment-strategy-side-stack](metric-assessment-strategy-side-stack.html) | Year marker, one metric intro card, one assessment model with repeated standalone centered ordinal+label factors, repeatable strategy cards, and repeatable sidebar notes; only an explicitly emphasized terminal target/outcome note may use the dark variant | Assessment ordinals are merged into labels or not centered, sidebar notes are merged into strategy cards, an ordinary first metric becomes the dominant dark card, or connectors dominate | Talent strategy with assessment and sidebar |
| [metric-highlight-quote-columns](metric-highlight-quote-columns.html) | Three peer columns; each column has two evidence subsections, one highlighted value/caption, and one outcome quote | Columns have different internal schemas, highlight/quote is absent, or content is a table | Incentive columns with highlight and quote |
| [summary-metric-peer-cards](summary-metric-peer-cards.html) | One ROI summary band with three columns followed by three same-kind ROI cards with ordinal, value, caption, and list | The summary is a peer card, cards lack ordinals or value/caption/list roles, or a table dominates | ROI summary plus peer evidence cards |
| [metric-summary-history-cards](metric-summary-history-cards.html) | One metric-leading summary band with bullet list followed by repeatable history cards with metric panels and bullets; paired metric comparisons retain two labels and one divider | Summary metric is card-owned, history cards lack metric panels, a paired comparison would be flattened into slash text, or card schemas differ | Historical metric summary and evidence cards |

Only the files linked above are valid catalog routes. For quantitative content, compare units and scales explicitly; never create bar widths from incompatible values.
